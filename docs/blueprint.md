# **App Name**: Gift Auto Mobile Detailing

## Core Features:

- Gallery: Display a gallery of before and after photos of auto detailing and house cleaning services.
- Testimonials: Showcase customer testimonials with star ratings, names, photos, service type, and feedback.
- Contact Form and Map: Enable users to submit inquiries via a contact form and display contact information, including a Google Map of the service area.
- Booking/Scheduling: Provide a clean, functional booking/scheduling page with service type selection, date/time pickers, address input with map autofill, contact details, and payment information.
- Sentiment Summarizer: Based on the customer's past reviews and submitted testimonials, this tool offers a summary of the general opinion towards the quality of service.  It can optionally display a counter of total 5-star ratings and average total of received score.
- About Us Section: Detail the founder's story with Christian faith elements, team introductions, and community outreach information.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated teal (#0f766e), echoing the original request.
- Background color: Very light, desaturated teal (#f0f4f4).
- Accent color: Analogous, brighter, desaturated blue (#38bdf8).
- Headings: 'Playfair', a modern serif.
- Body: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif.
- Use rounded, outlined icons to complement the rounded buttons.
- Use subtle fade-in and slide-reveal animations for content display.
